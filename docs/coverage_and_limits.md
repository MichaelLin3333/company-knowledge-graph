# Coverage, uncertainty and limits

The graph is an evidence registry. A larger network provides more hypotheses, not proof of financial dependence, market causality or a delayed trading opportunity.

## What coverage can mean

For each declared batch, count the required document slots, sections reviewed, dated list entries extracted, identities resolved, known leads investigated and accepted claims with traceable evidence. Report denominators for those declared tasks only.

For example, extracting all 200 entries from a specific dated list is complete extraction of that list. It does not establish that those are all suppliers, that they serve every product, that the relationships remain operating, or that their stocks are independent observations. (The number 200 is illustrative.)

Branch states are not researched, pending, reviewed with findings, reviewed with no explicit evidence, blocked, or not applicable with a reason. A no-finding state describes the searched documents; it does not assert that no relationship exists.

## Important distinctions

| Evidence says | It does not establish |
|---|---|
| A company is on a supplier list | Component assignment, volume, exclusivity or revenue share |
| A company makes camera modules | It supplies this customer's camera module |
| A private company received an investment | A public share price for the private company |
| Two companies are in one project | A bilateral contract between them |
| A project has an investment headline | Committed finance, financial close or deployed capital |
| A contract has a ceiling | Actual procurement or recognized revenue |
| An acquisition is announced | Completed ownership or operational consolidation |
| A stake is disclosed today | The same ownership percentage years earlier |
| Two share classes/ADRs trade | Two independent company confirmations |
| A relationship is documented | A causal stock-price response or transmission lag |

## Temporal limits

The current graph, research revision, evidence publication cutoff, operating interval and price snapshot are different concepts. An evidence filter excludes unknown or too-new publication dates, while a revision captures what the registry contained at a save point. Neither reconstructs missing historical disclosures automatically.

Quantities are claim-specific. A later filing can update an exposure number without changing the earlier date when the basic relationship became public. Keep these vintages separate. Planned effective dates may be later than the public-information cutoff when the plan was already announced.

## Provenance limits

Primary explicit describes a source class, not audited completeness, guaranteed accuracy or economic importance. Attributed explicit identifies the reporting institution or specialist rather than relabelling its claim as company-confirmed. Mirrored announcements are one origin, not multiple independent sources.

The initial migration preserves original atlas evidence, exclusions, source access gaps and unresolved direction. Legacy discovery paths must never become reviewed economic arrows automatically. New corrections should improve those records through sourced claims and explicit updates.

## Display and price limits

The bounded graph hides additional results to remain readable. Hidden results remain in the relationship table; hidden counts are display counts, not research gaps. Node sizes and edge thickness do not encode financial materiality.

Yfinance quotations may be delayed, absent or a latest daily close. Inspect the observation and retrieval timestamps, currency/unit and stale/error flags. Current quotes remain current when an historical evidence filter is selected. A private company has no substituted parent/investor quote.

The framework does not run statistical experiments, price-based connection discovery, trading recommendations or portfolio accounting. Those require a separately authorized study with its own timing, data and inference protocol.
