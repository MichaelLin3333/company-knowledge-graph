# Research framework guide

- **Playbook:** the complete company-neutral research method, six branches, evidence rules, bounded traversal and source routing.
- **Research workflow:** ready-to-copy company/project requests, validation/publication and resuming the frontier.
- **Batch format:** machine-readable object meanings, dates, claim supersession and explicit record updates.
- **Coverage and limits:** how to interpret list extraction, evidence gaps, historical filters and prices.

Reusable machine-readable materials live in `research/`, `templates/` and `schemas/`. The fictional examples are tests, not research additions.
