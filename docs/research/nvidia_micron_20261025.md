# NVIDIA and Micron: memory, manufacturing and capacity commitments

Research cutoff: **25 October 2026**. This is the fourth research expansion after the original atlas, prepared against revision 4. It adds **11 companies, 36 relationship records, 64 atomic claims, 24 sources and one project**. Company nodes remain separate from products, facilities and securities.

The strongest new paths connect Micron's equipment and manufacturing partners to specific memory generations, then connect those products to NVIDIA platforms. These are documented economic relationships. **This batch measures no stock response or latency.**

## 3. Four different Micron—Anthropic roles

The [22 June 2026 original announcement](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-and-Anthropic-Announce-Strategic-Agreement-to-Scale-Next-Generation-AI-Infrastructure/default.aspx) explicitly supports four separate records:

1. Micron supplies data-center memory/storage under an agreement.
2. Both companies collaborate on AI memory/storage architecture.
3. Anthropic's Claude is deployed inside Micron.
4. Micron has invested in Anthropic's Series H financing round.

Investment amount, stake, governance rights, hardware quantities and customer-specific payments are undisclosed. Anthropic remains a private company without an own public-share quotation. These overlapping roles make a single generic “partner” label inadequate; they also complicate later causal interpretation.

## 5. Tongluo: an asset transaction with changing forecasts

Micron [announced completion on15 March 2026](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Completes-Acquisition-of-PSMCs-Tongluo-P5-Site-in-Taiwan-03-15-2026/default.aspx) of its acquisition of **PSMC's Tongluo P5 fab/site**. This does not make listed PSMC a Micron subsidiary. The graph retains two companies and one physical project record, with Micron as acquirer/developer and PSMC as former owner/seller.

Micron's [FY 2026 annual, Item 2 p.50](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm) gives **USD 1.8bn cash consideration**. That term uses the annual's 9 October 2026 availability; the March completion claim retains its earlier date.

The March release targeted meaningful shipments in fiscal 2028. Later [Q3 p.6](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q3/Q3-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf) and [Q4 p.3](https://s25.q4cdn.com/621799436/files/doc_financials/2026/q4/Q4-FY26-Prepared-Remarks.pdf) remarks target **mid-calendar 2027**. Both forecasts remain visible with their dates. Neither is recorded as achieved production. Local title entities, contractors and financing remain pending; no project SPV is invented.
