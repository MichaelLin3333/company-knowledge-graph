## Findings that improve the research basket

**Kioxia: named memory-business exposure.** The latest annual securities report names Apple Group. It reports ¥476,014 million in sales, **20.4%** of consolidated revenue, for the year ended 31 March 2026; the comparable prior-year figures are ¥300,512 million and **17.6%**. These are consolidated customer measures. They do not identify a particular Apple model, NAND part, shipment route or invoice issuer. The original filing is dated 24 June 2026, but the English translation’s first-public date has not been independently established. Its new relationship is conservatively excluded from historical-cutoff queries until that timing is resolved. [Kioxia original report, PDF pp.23 and 87](https://www.kioxia-holdings.com/content/dam/kioxia-hd/en-jp/ir/library/securities/asset/Annual-Securities-Report-FY2025-EN.pdf).

**Japan Display: a relationship can shrink sharply without disappearing.** The 23 June 2026 filing reports Apple Group sales of ¥3,345 million, **2.5%** of total sales, for the year ended 31 March 2026, against ¥30,587 million and **16.3%** a year earlier. That is evidence against treating the old supplier roster as a constant exposure matrix. It does not establish termination or a stock-price delay. The named-customer edge is dated; the generic roster edge remains undated. [Japan Display original filing, PDF p.28](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/6740/yuho_pdf/S100YH91/00.pdf).

**CosMX: two explicitly described battery channels.** Its FY2025 annual report names Apple in notebook/tablet customer cooperation and among existing smartphone customers. The graph keeps those roles separate. Its top-five customer table remains anonymous: the largest customer accounts for 19.42% and the top five for 55.12%, but neither figure is assigned to Apple. No Apple model or precise battery allocation is supplied by this evidence. [CosMX annual report, PDF pp.6, 15–16 and 32](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-31/1225052082.PDF).

**Kersen: structural manufacturing beyond chips.** The annual report names Apple among customers served by its precision structural-parts and related assembly business. The broad offering includes metal/plastic processes. Examples of enclosures, frames, buttons and hinges are not each assigned to Apple. Its anonymous top-five concentration of 47.68% remains separate from the named customer connection. [Kersen annual report, PDF pp.11 and 19–20](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-23/1225156920.PDF).

**NOK: the supplier entity and the quoted parent differ.** The completed 1 October 2026 joint share transfer created listed NOK Group, code 641A, as NOK Corporation’s wholly owning parent. NOK Corporation continues as a separate operating subsidiary. The app therefore gives 641A.T to the parent, leaves the operating subsidiary without a current own-share instrument, and does not invent a direct NOK Group–Apple supplier edge. The two entities share an economic confirmation group. [Original establishment disclosure, PDF pp.1–2](https://ssl4.eir-parts.net/doc/641A/tdnet/2890262/00.pdf).

**Nanofilm and JX: former names do not create new companies.** Nanofilm’s original prospectus explicitly traces Pte Ltd to Limited in October 2020. JX’s official history records English-name changes in 2023 and 2024; its current listing is 5016. ENEOS in Apple’s parenthetical is not merged with JX. [Nanofilm prospectus, PDF pp.42 and 305](https://links.sgx.com/FileOpen/Nanofilm%20Technologies%20International%20Limited%20-%20Prospectus%20%2823%20Oct%202020%29.ashx?App=IPO&FileID=6228), [JX official history](https://www.jx-nmm.com/english/company/history.html), [JX listing FAQ](https://www.jx-nmm.com/english/ir/faq.html).

For Baoji, Tony, Sanan, Renesas and LX Semicon, the reviewed portfolios do not establish an Apple-specific part. No titanium grade, LED, SiC substrate, charging material or display IC is allocated to Apple merely by combining a catalogue with the roster. Wingtech’s historical roster membership does not certify current ownership of the assets that originally served Apple. These are recorded research questions.

Most new sources have unresolved first-public dates. URL paths, report years, print dates and profile-update dates are not substitutes. Taiwan exchange profiles generated on 26 October local time were retrieved while it was still 25 October in New York; only identity fields were used. Date uncertainty is visible in the app, and unknown-date connections do not pass historical-cutoff filters.
