The note reports year-end 2025 holding-group balances of **EUR 1,907.5m loans receivable** and **EUR 1,191.9m advances**. These are balances, not new annual disbursements or standalone-entity allocations. The operating company's parent field is corrected to the holding partnership; its original imported record remains in history.

This branch gives later research a concrete mechanism: supplier capacity, financing and investment value can transmit different shocks. It establishes no stock-return timing.

## 2. PSMC is more than a fab seller

PSMC's [FY2025 annual, PDF p.48 / printed p.45](https://www.powerchip.com/upload/media/financials/annual_reports/Annual_Reports_2025_CH.pdf) explicitly describes two reciprocal relationships: PSMC's long-term advanced-DRAM post-wafer manufacturing for Micron, and Micron assistance with PSMC's niche DRAM technology. The shareholder letter also names PWF and HBM advanced packaging. Exact production volumes, products, contractual subsidiaries and allocation to any NVIDIA platform remain unknown.

These roles are separate from the Tongluo asset sale. They do not create a direct PSMC—NVIDIA supply edge. The annual's first public availability is not independently established, so the new contract claims remain excluded from dated graph filters.

The [17 January 2026 original Micron LOI](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Signs-Letter-of-Intent-to-Purchase-Tongluo-Site-Begin-Strategic-Partnership-with-PSMC-01-17-2026/default.aspx) already proposed **USD 1.8bn** consideration and outlined the intended reciprocal relationship. The earlier batch's October annual-report claim concerns the completed acquisition's cash consideration. Both remain: **a proposed amount in January is different from completed payment**. No closing or production milestone is backdated.

The PSMC report also names Tata Electronics as a technology-collaboration lead. That adjacent relationship is queued for its own verification; it is not assigned to Micron's products.
