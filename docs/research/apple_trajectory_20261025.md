# Apple manufacturing: completeness, packaging and changing business ownership

Research cutoff: **25 October 2026**. Published research batch: `apple-trajectory-20261025-v1`, following revision 6. This report answers whether the Apple example is complete enough to move directly into the proposed correlation experiment.

**Decision: continue research.** The documented manufacturing map is incomplete, and a historically eligible statistical basket has not yet been frozen. No prices were downloaded and no correlation, signal or trading experiment ran in this batch.

## What “complete” means here

Apple's current [supply-chain report directory](https://www.supplychainreports.apple/home/default.aspx) still links to a **FY2023** supplier roster. It covers 98% of that fiscal year's direct materials, manufacturing and assembly spend. That scope does not describe all suppliers, every product component, current contracts or Apple's entire corporate network. We rechecked the live link; the returned document remains the 27-page FY2023 list.

All **187 entries** were already extracted. This batch resolves **seven more identities**, taking the cumulative register from **47 to 54 resolved**, with **133 pending**. This is a count of reconciled names, not a percentage of Apple production or economic importance. Exact matches to existing Tripod and TSMT nodes are reused; they are not new companies or additional independent confirmations.

The batch investigates eight companies within the 25-company/two-hop limit. It adds **seven company nodes, five instruments, 18 relationship records and 30 atomic claims**. Five latest annual reports receive relevant-section reviews, while two private-company/report-body gaps remain explicit. The prior three Apple annual and two interim section reviews are reused at the same cutoff after checking the filing inventory.

## A supplier can change without its ticker changing

Knowles [announced the completed sale](https://www.knowles.com/resources/education/blogs/post/press-releases/2024/12/30/knowles-corporation-completes-the-sales-of-its-consumer-mems-microphone-business-to-syntiant) of its consumer MEMS microphone business to Syntiant on **30 December 2024**. The later annual report identifies 27 December as closing. This was a **business disposal**, not an acquisition of Knowles Corporation.

Knowles's [FY2025 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1587523/000158752326000005/kn-20251231.htm), released 9 February 2026, also reports continuing Syntiant connections: preferred shares carried at **$83.4 million**, a **$5.9 million** junior note at year-end, **$17.3 million** of annual sales transactions, and facility leases/subleases. These are separate economic channels. The preferred carrying value is not an ownership percentage, and the note balance is not a new advance. The graph therefore retains four distinct post-sale roles alongside the business acquisition record.

**Implication for the eventual experiment:** Knowles's older Apple-list membership cannot automatically represent unchanged consumer-microphone operating exposure after the disposal. It also does not prove that all Apple ties ended. An Apple-to-Syntiant edge needs separate explicit evidence; none is created here.

Syntiant [announced a proposed IPO](https://www.syntiant.com/press-release/syntiant-publicly-files-registration-statement-for-proposed-initial-public-offering/) on 6 July 2026. The SEC inventory returned registration amendments, most recently 31 August, without evidence sufficient here to confirm completed public trading. The proposed **SYTN** symbol is not admitted as a tradable instrument. It is also distinct from Synthomer's **SYNT**. Syntiant has no public-share quotation in this graph; completion/listing verification remains queued.

## Remaining manufacturing trajectory

Documented company roles now span chips, memory, optics, displays, acoustics, batteries, glass, antennas, enclosures, packaging and final assembly. The map still lacks many **named links between these stages**. In particular:

- Connectors and passives: generic supplier memberships exceed verified Apple-specific component assignments.
- Materials and coatings: YUTO's process connection is explicit, but named input vendors and their product allocation are missing.
- Tooling, testing and logistics: named commercial routes and their historical validity remain patchy.
- Assembly handoffs: a component supplier and an assembler appearing separately in Apple's disclosures do not prove deliveries between them.
- Business continuity: acquisitions, disposals and corporate reorganizations require period-specific issuer and operating-business treatment.

Catcher's report gives a useful generic process sequence: material forming, secondary machining, then surface treatment. This remains company context. It does not establish a specific Apple raw-material source, product model or downstream factory.

## Gate for starting the first experiment

Complete discovery of a confidential global supply chain is unattainable from public documents. We do **not** need to resolve every supplier before studying a declared subset. The appropriate stopping point is a fixed, auditable basket whose own eligibility passes these checks:

| Gate | Current assessment | Required before a confirmatory test |
|---|---|---|
| Declared scope and basket | Not frozen | Choose named production stages, counterparties, dates and exclusion rules before looking at returns. |
| Issuer and security identity | Partial across the overall Apple map | One economic issuer per observation; separate subsidiaries, parents, local shares and ADRs. |
| Public availability and valid relationship period | Partial | Evidence must predate included observations; resolve material business transfers and unknown publication dates, or exclude affected windows. |
| Product/materiality claims | Partial | Product roles need explicit support. Unknown exposure may remain unknown with predeclared equal weighting; anonymous concentrations cannot supply weights. |
| Price and exchange-calendar coverage | Not attempted in this batch | Audit actual yfinance availability, holidays, closures, adjustments and missing bars for the fixed universe. |
| Statistical design and confirmation | Protocol exists; Apple inputs not frozen | Lock the first experiment, matched controls, horizons, testing family and fresh confirmation period; assess power and false discoveries. |

Unknown materiality need not exclude a company automatically. Current inclusion evidence must, however, support the hypothesis actually being tested. A recent disclosure cannot justify a retrospective 2015 connection, and daily calendar labels cannot establish hourly latency across non-overlapping exchanges.

The next research priority is named component/assembly routing and historical continuity for a **specific Apple basket**, while the unresolved register remains available for later expansion. When the gate passes, the first chat about the experiment should explain only that experiment's inputs, method, result and implication, using yfinance prices.
