# Apple supplier roster: all 187 names reviewed

Research cutoff:25 October 2026, America/New_York. Publication is a new revision; earlier batches and the statistical study are preserved.

The 52 names left from the previous batch have now received a first-pass review. Every one of the 187 names in Apple’s FY2023 supplier roster is represented in the company graph. This completes enumeration and first-pass review of that dated list; it does not complete Apple’s current supply chain, a confidential bill of materials, or every company’s six research branches.

This batch adds **54 company records,59 relationship records,121 atomic claims and 73 sources**. The complete graph contains **312 companies and 492 relationship records**. Apple’s direct neighborhood contains **201 company records including Apple** (200 counterparties), of which 199 are not marked ceased by merger. These are records, not 201 independent tradable stocks. Parent, group, former-name and legal-entity distinctions remain material.

## What the review establishes

- Apple’s roster explicitly names each retained supplier. That establishes dated membership; it does not allocate a component or measure consolidated sales exposure.
- Interplex Holdings is explicitly described as ENNOVI Holdings’ former name. A2015 SGX filing similarly documents Molex Incorporated’s conversion into Molex LLC. Each continuity chain remains one company record.
- Boyd completed the sale of its thermal business to Eaton in March 2026 while its engineered-materials business continued independently. The edge records a business disposal, not ownership of all Boyd.
- Lumileds’ proposed Sanan/Inari share acquisition was terminated in April 2026. Its August 2026 transaction with Gallant concerns senior secured loans and is separately labelled as financing.
- VARTA’s October 2026 notice names Microbattery in opened insolvency proceedings with business operations continuing. VARTA Consumer Batteries is explicitly outside those proceedings.
- Western Digital’s 2026 filing records its completed Sandisk separation and says it held no Sandisk common shares by 3 July 2026. No Apple–Sandisk supplier edge is manufactured from the former WD business.

## Identity results and remaining gaps

Of the 52 newly reviewed names,36 are reconciled within the stated document scope, one is an identified historical entity (Trinseo S.A.), eight are group-level mappings, two retain continuity questions, and five remain named-only identities. The earlier 135 reconciliations are preserved at their original scope; they have not all received a new legal audit in this batch.

The five named-only records are **CN Innovations Holdings, Global Lighting Technologies, Golden Arrow Printing, Sunny Optical Technology and Suzhou Zhongjiemai**. Cosmosupplylab and Shenzhen Forceblack retain continuity/registry questions. Group-level limits apply to ASE, Broadcom, IDEMIA, Luen Fung, Paishing, Quadrant, Sony and Zhenghe. Their explicit roster relationships remain visible, with unknown legal registration, exact contracting entity or current status labelled where appropriate.

The generic Global Lighting and Sunny labels are not assigned 4935.TW or 2382.HK automatically. General Interface Solution Limited in Samoa is kept separate from its 6456.TW listed holding and the Taiwan operating company. Broadcom’s old Singapore name is not treated as legal issuer equivalence with Broadcom Inc. Sony’s 2021 name reuse prevents an automatic match of the FY2023 literal name to a specific operating subsidiary.

## Coverage and evidence boundaries

The original 187-entry list and all former exclusions remain available in the cumulative identity register. Identity and continuity checks are distinct from full company business reviews. Selected legal, financial and relevant business sections were examined for 21 investigation targets; latest-three-annual/latest-two-interim reviews for every newly enumerated supplier remain pending. Apple’s prior relevant-section review at this same cutoff is reused. No percentage of the true network discovered is claimed.

Sources include company disclosures, exchange/regulator profiles, original financial statements, legal/certification records and explicitly attributed reporting. The source register records publication precision, retrieval time, original URL and cache hash when available. Original publisher documents hosted by mirrors are identified as mirrors. Some cache and browser routes were blocked; the download manifest preserves those attempts. Where only Apple’s name is established, no additional legal or product fact is invented.
