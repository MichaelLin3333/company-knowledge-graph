# Arvida: the project partnership behind ELYSIS deployment

Research cutoff **25 October 2026** · Third published research expansion · One project investigated · Project mode, with company identity and participant resolution only.

This batch follows the existing ELYSIS financing frontier into the **Arvida 100kA demonstration plant**. It resolves two additional legal entities and four explicit relationships. The facility, the plant partnership and ELYSIS LP remain separate records. The earlier Alma 450kA project is unchanged. This is evidence research; no return, latency or trading experiment was run.

## 1. The useful discovery: separate the technology company from the plant

Québec's legal instrument names **Usine de démonstration de la Technologie ELYSIS S.E.C.**, a Montréal-based limited partnership, as the intended recipient of a unit subscription. The decree authorizes Investissement Québec to administer up to **CAD 140 million**. It was adopted on **26 June 2024** and published in the gazette on **17 July 2024**. The named legal recipient is admitted from the publication date; adoption is not assumed to be public availability. Payment and closing were not established. [Decree 1022-2024, pp.5103–5104](https://www.publicationsduquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fileadmin/gazette/pdf_encrypte/lois_reglements/2024F/83646.pdf).

Rio Tinto's latest annual report gives the same partnership's English name, **ELYSIS Technology Demonstration Plant L.P.** Its footnote **(r)** identifies two particular legal companies as partners: **Rio Tinto Alcan Inc.** and **9519-2845 Quebec inc.** The second is a separately named Canadian group subsidiary, not an alias for Rio Tinto Alcan. The report gives **74.3% effective Group ownership** for the plant LP. This is a group figure with the CEDS definition on printed p.230; the adjacent 100% number belongs to a separate class-of-units-held column. Neither number establishes either company's individual stake or Investissement Québec's percentage. [Annual Report 2025, printed pp.230,232,237–238](https://cdn-rio.dataweavers.io/-/media/content/documents/invest/reports/annual-reports/2025-annual-report.pdf?rev=bc8724dee4cf437fbfcdd0ff876df06c).

| New explicit connection | What the source establishes | What remains unknown |
|---|---|---|
| Investissement Québec → Arvida plant LP | Authorized subscription in the named partnership | Paid amount, closing, partner class and current percentage |
| Rio Tinto Alcan Inc. → Arvida plant LP | Specifically named partnership interest at FY2025 | Individual percentage and general/limited-partner role |
| 9519-2845 Quebec inc. → Arvida plant LP | Another specifically named partnership interest | Individual percentage, immediate parent and function |
| Investissement Québec → ELYSIS LP | A separate technology-company subscription mandate | Cash drawn and full round completion |

Arrows in this table describe a documented legal or funding role. The application keeps one arrow-free canvas connection per company pair; details retain direction and every separate claim. Shared project participation creates no extra bilateral contract.

## 2. Read the financing figures as a dated sequence

The original plant announcement described **CAD 375m / USD 285m** total investment. It attributed **CAD 235m / USD 179m** to Rio Tinto and **CAD 140m / USD 106m** to Québec through IQ as equity partners. Each CAD/USD pair describes the same announcement. These contribution amounts are not a disclosed ownership split, nor evidence of money already paid. The release names Rio Tinto at group level; the later legal partners are recorded without allocating that group contribution between them. [Rio Tinto, 28 June 2024](https://www.riotinto.com/en/news/releases/2024/rio-tinto-to-install-carbon-free-aluminium-smelting-cells-using-first-elysistm-technology-licence).

| Record | Amount and instrument | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| June 2024 plant announcement | CAD 375m original total; equity partner contributions described | Historical announced investment |
| July 2024 gazette | Up to CAD 140m of plant-LP units through IQ | Authorized ceiling; payment unverified |
| January 2025 ELYSIS announcement | Up to CAD 14,117,095 of technology-company shares | Separate recipient and round |
| May 2026 federal announcement | CAD 100m support for a CAD 440m project | Announced support; exact instrument and cash drawn unresolved |

On **29 May 2026**, ISED announced **CAD 100m** support for Rio Tinto Alcan's **CAD 440m** demonstration project. The reviewed release does not specify grant, debt or equity terms, repayment conditions or disbursements. It does not reconcile the change from CAD 375m to CAD 440m or explain whether federal support replaces or supplements other commitments. The table therefore is not a sources-and-uses statement. The associated BDC industry program is not recorded as an Arvida loan. [ISED announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/05/minister-joly-announces-100-million-investment-to-transform-canadas-aluminum-sector.html).

The separate **10 January 2025** ministry release describes a **CAD 400m ELYSIS funding round** and up to CAD 14,117,095 intended to preserve Québec's **3.53% holding in ELYSIS**. These figures are not plant ownership or plant cost. The later gazetted mandate names **Elysis société en commandite**, not the Arvida project partnership. Its adoption date is 18 December 2024; the announcement provides earlier public evidence on 10 January 2025 and the decree itself was published on 15 January. [Original ministry release distributed by CNW](https://www.newswire.ca/fr/news-releases/une-production-d-aluminium-plus-durable-au-quebec-jusqu-a-14-1-m-pour-poursuivre-le-developpement-d-elysis-md--874992267.html), [decree 1805-2024](https://www.publicationsduquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fileadmin/gazette/pdf_encrypte/lois_reglements/2025F/84755.pdf).

## 3. The plan changed, while production remains a target

The original plan was ten 100kA cells, up to 2,500 tonnes per year, and first production by 2027. The latest annual report's 2026 action column refers to implementing **the first seven cells**, with first hot metal expected in 2027. Its operating 100kA research cell is separate from these new cells. This distinction prevents a research-cell result being treated as completion of the new plant. [Original announcement](https://www.riotinto.com/en/news/releases/2024/rio-tinto-to-install-carbon-free-aluminium-smelting-cells-using-first-elysistm-technology-licence), [Annual Report 2025, printed p.62](https://cdn-rio.dataweavers.io/-/media/content/documents/invest/reports/annual-reports/2025-annual-report.pdf?rev=bc8724dee4cf437fbfcdd0ff876df06c).

The regional project page inspected at the cutoff describes **seven initial cells with three possible additions**, CAD 440m project cost and 2027 first metal. Its body figures say “as of 11 September 2026.” Page metadata gives creation on 20 August and modification on 8 October; neither proves when each current passage first appeared. The undated current-version claim is therefore excluded from precise historical eligibility. The dated annual already supports the seven-cell plan. No seven-cell achieved output is inferred by scaling the original maximum capacity. [Regional project page](https://www.votreriotintoslsj.com/projets/elysis/).

The CNESST matching project row names **Rio Tinto Alcan inc.** as **maître d'œuvre**, the construction principal for that notice. This is useful legal-role evidence, not proof of asset title or an external EPC contract. The fetched register version is dated 16 January 2026 even though the URL includes an older cache query. Its exact publication time is unresolved; the March 2025 notice-receipt and planned-opening dates are not substituted for publication. [CNESST register, PDF p.24, notice OUV646767](https://www.cnesst.gouv.qc.ca/sites/default/files/documents/liste-chantiers-necessistant-rss-temps-plein-2025_.pdf?cid=1760454635).

## 4. The operating links are richer than an ownership chart

| Company | Project role retained | Limit on interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Plant LP | Named development partnership and authorized subscription recipient | Full partnership agreement and asset title pending |
| Investissement Québec | Authorized equity-subscription administrator | Administration does not establish full disbursement |
| Rio Tinto Alcan | Named partner; construction principal in matching notice | Individual stake and precise licence contract pending |
| 9519-2845 Quebec inc. | Named partner | No general-partner designation inferred |
| ELYSIS LP | Technology provider/licensor | Group-level licence announcement does not identify exact legal licensee |
| Alcoa | Planned electrode-materials manufacture; optional purchaser | Delivery allocation and exercise remain unknown |

Alcoa's release states that it will manufacture proprietary ELYSIS anodes and cathodes at its Technical Center and has the right to purchase **up to 40%** of Arvida demonstration metal. Rio Tinto describes the option over the **first four production years**. The percentage is a purchase ceiling, not a plant stake, executed delivery volume or guaranteed demand. The reviewed sources do not establish an Alcoa-to-plant-SPV purchase agreement, so that bilateral edge is not invented. ELYSIS retains its technology IP. [Alcoa, 28 June 2024](https://news.alcoa.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2024/Alcoa-Announces-Agreement-on-Industrial-Scale-Demonstration-of-ELYSIS-Carbon-Free-Smelting-Technology/default.aspx), [Rio Tinto option description](https://www.riotinto.com/en/news/releases/2024/rio-tinto-to-install-carbon-free-aluminium-smelting-cells-using-first-elysistm-technology-licence).

These roles suggest distinct mechanisms for later hypothesis design: licensing revenue or technical milestones; electrode demand; capital requirements and public support; optional metal allocation. Financial materiality and delayed stock pricing remain unestablished. Apple has no verified role in this specific plant from these sources; the existing Apple–ELYSIS funding and purchase records do not manufacture one.
