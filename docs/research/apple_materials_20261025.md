# Apple materials: identities, upstream inputs and industrial funding

Issued 25 October 2026 · Evidence cutoff 25 October 2026 · Batch `apple-materials-20261025-v1` · Base revision 2

This second research batch adds **18 companies/legal entities, 23 relationships, 45 atomic claims and one project**. After publication, the graph contains **153 companies and 270 relationships**. The additions extend the first manufacturing batch into materials and financial participation while resolving ten more names from Apple's dated supplier list.

The strongest additions are the ELYSIS ownership/funding distinction, Henkel's explicitly named upstream suppliers, and Lens Technology's named Apple customer disclosure. Generic supplier-list membership remains useful discovery evidence, with its component assignments and current commercial exposure still unknown.

## Research inputs and method

The starting point was revision 2 and its pending supplier identities. Seven companies were selected for deeper review: Apple, Henkel, Amphenol, Lens Technology, Alcoa, ELYSIS and Rio Tinto Alcan. Twelve other entities received identity or context resolution only. This distinction keeps company enumeration separate from completed ecosystem research.

Apple's filing inventory still lists FY2025 as its latest annual filing and Q3/Q2 FY2026 as the two latest interim filings. The previous three annual and two interim relevant-section reviews were reused after that inventory check. They were not counted as five newly read reports. [Apple filing inventory](https://investor.apple.com/sec-filings/default.aspx)

Original annual-report sections were reviewed for Henkel, Amphenol and Lens. The original Alcoa investment note was accessible, while its full filing download was blocked; the review therefore remains partial. No public standalone ELYSIS annual financial report was obtained. Rio Tinto Alcan's identity was confirmed, but its private report and the latest group annual proxy remain an access gap. These limitations are retained in the coverage register.

For identity resolution, the research used issuer stock pages, the Vienna, Taiwan and Korean exchange registers, the HKEX filing inventory and original company disclosures. It retained the distinction between a corporate group named in a commercial announcement and an exact contracting subsidiary. No stock-price performance was used to select companies, and no statistical or trading experiment ran.

## 1. ELYSIS: purchases, support and ownership are different

Apple's March 2022 announcement identifies an aluminium purchase for intended use in the iPhone SE. A separate Alcoa disclosure explicitly describes Apple as a **non-equity funding partner** at ELYSIS's launch. Apple's earlier technical-support announcement provides a third role. These become three relationships between the same company pair, shown as one connection on the canvas. [Apple purchase](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/03/apples-four-point-seven-billion-in-green-bonds-support-innovative-green-technology/), [Alcoa funding disclosure](https://aacomnoa.apps.alcoa.com/sustainability/stories/releases?id=2022%2F03%2Fapple-to-use-elysis-zero-carbon-aluminum-for-the-latest-iphone-se), [Apple technical support](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2018/05/apple-paves-the-way-for-breakthrough-carbon-free-aluminum-smelting-method/)

The public filing date is 26 February 2026; the holdings relate to the reporting period, rather than every date since the venture began. [Original investment note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1675149/000119312526077167/R19.htm), [filing date](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1675149/000119312526077167/0001193125-26-077167-index.html)

ELYSIS's June 2024 release reports aggregate investment of **CAD650 million**, with an approximate **65% private / 35% public funding mix**. **Implication:** the network now separates a customer, a development supporter, corporate owners and a materials collaborator. It establishes possible channels of economic exposure, without measuring each channel's materiality or delayed pricing.

## 2. Henkel: an explicitly documented second tier

Henkel's November 2025 supplier awards explicitly name Synthomer, Dow and Kolon Industries. All three named award recipients were enumerated and resolved. The announcement describes an adhesives-portfolio collaboration with Synthomer, innovation across Adhesive Technologies with Dow, and supply continuity/inventory resilience with Kolon. [Original supplier disclosure](https://www.henkel.com/press-and-media/press-releases-and-kits/2025-11-24-henkel-recognizes-its-strategic-partners-for-best-in-class-performance-across-the-value-chains-2101224)

The graph therefore contains the independently documented path **supplier → Henkel → Apple**. Henkel's Apple connection in this batch is FY2023 list membership. It does not identify a particular Apple adhesive. The graph creates no Synthomer–Apple, Dow–Apple or Kolon–Apple supply edge and does not assign those firms' deliveries to Apple orders.

Henkel's latest annual-report business and procurement sections were reviewed separately. Its product range helps organize future questions; it cannot resolve an Apple bill of materials. Dow is represented at the listed group level because the award announcement does not specify the immediate contracting subsidiary. [Henkel annual report](https://www.henkel.com/resource/blob/2131544/e5b32595fccb9c899816cfb539438f41/data/2025-annual-report.pdf)

**Implication:** there is now a defensible route for researching upstream chemicals and ingredients. The missing link is transaction/product allocation, so a two-hop path should not be treated as a quantified Apple dependency.

## 3. Lens Technology: a named customer with anonymous concentration

Lens's FY2025 annual report names Apple among 16 customers/brands in its strategic-customer discussion. The full 16-name passage is enumerated; the other 15 names remain identity/contract-route leads. Similar brand names are not automatically matched to listed parents already in the graph. [Lens annual report, PDF p.21](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0428/2026042802531.pdf)

The same report gives the largest customer's sales share as **45.01%** and the five largest customers' combined share as **81.64%**, while leaving their ranked identities anonymous. Neither measure is assigned to Apple. Its anonymous largest-supplier figure likewise does not establish a particular supplier. These are company-level claims, separate from the named Apple relationship. [Concentration tables, PDF pp.27,81,121](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0428/2026042802531.pdf)

Apple's August 2016 disclosure explicitly identifies Lens glass production for Apple. That dated role is retained separately from the newer named customer relationship; it does not establish every current model or a 2026 component allocation. The Lens report also identifies its private immediate/ultimate holding company, Lens Technology (HK) Co., Ltd. This legal entity is distinct from the listed issuer's H-share instrument. [Apple glass disclosure](https://www.apple.com/ae/newsroom/2016/08/17Apple-Announces-Environmental-Progress-in-China-Applauds-Supplier-Commitment-to-Clean-Energy/), [parent and listing information, PDF pp.5,97](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0428/2026042802531.pdf)

**Implication:** the customer link is stronger than a generic supplier-list entry, but the named customer and anonymous exposure table cannot be combined to produce an Apple revenue estimate.

## 4. Manufacturing project: one operating demonstration, specific gaps

The new project record is **ELYSIS's 450kA industrial demonstration cell at Alma, Québec**. ELYSIS announced startup in November 2025 and said testing would continue to support future commercial deployment. The recorded 450kA quantity is electrical current rating, not tonnes of output, sales or financial commitment. [Original startup disclosure](https://elysis.com/en/elysis-achieves-breakthrough-with-commercial-size-cell-a-first-in-aluminium-production-using-the)

Only ELYSIS's technology-development/testing participation is accepted for this specific asset. The announcement names the Rio Tinto host group, but this batch does not establish the exact legal owner/operator or project financier. Those roles stay in the project queue. Apple is not added as a participant merely because it supported the ELYSIS venture.

The **Arvida 100kA/10-pot demonstration** is a separate project lead. Its technology licence, project SPV, ownership and financing need their own investigation. ELYSIS's corporate ownership percentages must not be reused as the Arvida plant ownership split. [Dated Arvida licence disclosure](https://elysis.com/en/issuing-first-smelter-technology-licence)
