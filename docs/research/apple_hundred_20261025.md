Cutoff: **25 October 2026, America/New_York**. Batch: `apple-hundred-20261025-v1`, following revision 7.

**The requested size threshold is reached.** Apple now has **100 directly connected company counterparties**, making **101 company nodes including Apple**. One counterparty is the historical Qorvo, Inc. issuer, which ceased in a merger. Excluding that former issuer leaves **100 non-ceased company records including Apple**, with no duplicate share classes or ADRs in that count.

This is a graph-size milestone. It does **not** mean that Apple's full network, every current supplier contract or a statistically eligible basket is complete. Research stops here for this request. No prices, correlations or trading experiments ran.

## Stronger evidence: the RF demand channel

Qorvo's [FY2026 annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1604778/000162828026032873/rfmd-20260328.htm), filed **8 May 2026**, explicitly identifies Apple as its largest end customer. Apple-related sales through multiple contract manufacturers represented approximately **50% of FY2026 revenue** and **47% in FY2025**. Samsung represented approximately **10%** in FY2026. The stated product class is RF solutions for mobile devices.

This establishes a named end-customer channel, but the source does not identify the individual manufacturers handling those sales. No Qorvo-to-Foxconn or other assembler edge is manufactured from the aggregate disclosure. The Apple revenue channel is different from a claim that Apple directly paid those invoices.

### The stock and legal business changed

The [5 October 2026 closing filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1604778/000095010326015180/dp254466_8k.htm) describes two steps. Qorvo, Inc. first survived as a Skyworks subsidiary, then merged into a different legal entity, Comet Acquisition II, LLC. That surviving LLC was renamed **Qorvo Technologies, LLC**. The former Inc. therefore cannot simply be relabelled as the surviving company or treated as a currently owned, still-existing issuer.

[Nasdaq's corporate-action notice](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/TraderNews.aspx?id=ECA2026-702) gives **2 October 2026** as QRVO's last trading date. No current QRVO instrument or substituted SWKS price is added. The graph preserves the former issuer and a completed acquisition relationship, with the successor legal name clearly identified as context. Current ownership of the successor needs its own legal-entity record before further expansion.

For a future study, standalone Qorvo observations must stop at the appropriate trading boundary. A later Skyworks observation is not another return from the same security, and the old 50% figure is not automatically the combined group's Apple exposure.

## What the 100-company count includes

The count uses the complete snapshot, before the display cap. It includes public and private legal entities and historically disclosed relationships. Earlier atlas records retain their original evidence and unresolved economic-direction flags; this batch does not claim to have newly revalidated every legacy connection.

- **100 direct counterparties:** no multi-hop reachability is needed to reach this count.
- **101 company nodes including Apple:** one node per recorded company/legal issuer, not per instrument.
- **100 non-ceased company records including Apple:** the former Qorvo issuer is retained separately.
- **100 unique issuer-equivalence groups among those non-ceased records:** affiliation or control is not automatically issuer equivalence, and this is not a count of statistically independent return series.

The application keeps its **100-node/300-edge display limit**. One company from this 101-node neighborhood is therefore hidden in the initial full view. Hidden counts, the complete relationship table and the company directory retain access. A smaller batch view shows the additions; opening a drawer does not expand the network. A company roster with every direct relationship and source reference accompanies this report.

## What remains unfinished

There are **102 unresolved names** in the original FY2023 roster. Many resolved names still need current product scope, named assembly handoffs, exposure, original publication dates or relationship validity periods. Connectors, passives, coatings, tooling, logistics and private operating subsidiaries remain unevenly documented. Current listing verification does not fix older relationship dates.

The research queue preserves those gaps separately for each company and branch. An expanded holding-company name in the original list remains pending when an exact legal-issuer reconciliation was not sufficiently established; it is not merged into a similarly named operating company to increase the count.

The size stopping rule is satisfied, so further Apple expansion pauses at this delivery without labelling the network complete. The existing [experiment-readiness checklist](apple_trajectory_20261025.md) still applies: freeze a particular basket, classify explicit economic roles, reconcile public dates and business continuity, then audit actual yfinance/calendar coverage and lock the first statistical experiment. Unknown financial exposure may remain unknown under a declared weighting rule. No empirical conclusion follows from this atlas alone.
