# NVIDIA optics: investment forms, capacity rights and factories

26 October 2026 · Bounded company batch · Base revision 10

NVIDIA’s optical ecosystem contains overlapping roles: supplier capacity, product collaboration and strategic finance. This batch adds **seven explicit relationship records, 16 atomic claims, three project records and seven participant roles**, while reusing all existing company identities. It adds no price observations and runs no statistical experiment. These relationships do not establish delayed stock responses.

The scope is NVIDIA plus three deeply investigated counterparties: Coherent, Lumentum and Corning. Qorvo is an explicitly enumerated second-hop historical asset seller; its wider ecosystem was not investigated. Four companies remain below the 25-company limit, and every added company pair is within two hops of NVIDIA. No complete supplier, portfolio or confidential bill-of-materials census is claimed.

The later current holding is not determined here.

Corning’s [commercial release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-and-corning-announce-long-term-partnership-to-strengthen-us-manufacturing-for-ai-infrastructure) describes planned capacity expansion. Its [8-K, Item 3.02](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/24741/000120677426000273/glw4631061-8k.htm) specifies warrants over up to 15m common shares at USD180 and pre-funded warrants over up to 3m shares at USD0.0001. USD500m is the aggregate paid package price. Exercises, expiry provisions, current ownership and a future factory budget are separate questions. The “up to USD3.2bn” headline must not become a completed USD3.2bn equity cash flow.

## 2. Projects reveal an additional company path

### Lumentum Greensboro: an acquired facility with a future product ramp

The [26 March 2026 issuer announcement](https://s21.q4cdn.com/377324469/files/doc_news/Lumentum-Announces-New-U-S--Manufacturing-Facility-to-Produce-Advanced-Lasers-for-the-Worlds-Largest-AI-Data-Centers-2026.pdf) names **Qorvo as the site seller** and **NVIDIA as an intended customer**. It describes an already operational facility that will be retrofitted for 6-inch indium-phosphide laser production, with a forecast ramp in **mid-2028**. Existing operations are not evidence that the planned new products already ship at volume.

The latest annual’s Note 4 reports a **17 March 2026 acquisition close for USD38m cash**, covering facility assets and workforce. That transaction amount is not the full retrofit budget or a disclosed allocation of NVIDIA’s USD2bn investment. The annual names an anonymous transitional wafer customer and an approximately 15-month arrangement. It does not explicitly name that customer as Qorvo; no extra supply edge is created from the plausible match.

The graph therefore preserves a documented path from NVIDIA to Lumentum, then to a historical Qorvo asset sale. It does not create NVIDIA–Qorvo supply. The existing QORVO record has later legal-cessation/merger context; no current QRVO instrument, inherited parent price or duplicate company was added. The source calls the seller Qorvo at group level; the exact legal selling subsidiary remains unresolved.

There is also an unresolved measurement difference: the March release says **240,000 square feet**, while the annual’s Properties section describes **148,000 square feet** for the Greensboro manufacturing site. These may use different area definitions. Both descriptions and dates remain visible rather than choosing a preferred number.

### Coherent Sherman: proposed support is not cash received

The [16 June 2026 original release](https://www.coherent.com/news/press-releases/a-chip-letter-of-intent-for-50m-to-expand-world-leading-manufacturing-facility-for-ai-infrastructure) describes a CHIPS **letter of intent for up to USD50m** toward expanding the existing Sherman, Texas, 6-inch InP facility. It links the expansion with the NVIDIA partnership and announces a groundbreaking ceremony later that day. A later final award, disbursement, completed construction, individual contractors and NVIDIA’s exact project allocation were not verified in this tranche.

The release also identifies prior state/local support. Government agencies remain project context; they are not invented company nodes. Neither attendance at a ceremony nor shared project participation creates bilateral financing contracts.

### Corning: a program before a complete site-level map

The May commercial release describes three planned new factories in North Carolina and Texas and a **10x U.S. optical-connectivity capacity target**. This is a dated planned program, not a verified tenfold increase already in operation. Individual site/SPV identities, contractors, commissioning dates and financing allocations remain queued. The project record carries the program scope explicitly; it is not a physical-facility node masquerading as a company.

## 4. Research implications and next frontier

These links support separate hypotheses about optical-demand news, capacity scarcity, financial support and construction milestones. They do not prescribe a common positive stock response. A capital provider can simultaneously be a customer, and multiple customers may drive the same supplier. For example, Corning belongs to both Apple’s materials network and NVIDIA’s optical-connectivity network; its stock cannot automatically be treated as a pure Apple-demand proxy.

Next steps are to verify current security holdings/exercises, reconcile Greensboro area and retrofit milestones, identify explicitly named transitional counterparties, verify final Sherman incentives, resolve Corning’s individual sites and follow named equipment/materials suppliers. Unknown exposure stays unknown. No project participant clique, current factory output assumption or Apple/NVIDIA price-causality arrow was manufactured.
