# NVIDIA financing: commitments, ownership and execution

Issued 26 October 2026. Published revision **15**.

NVIDIA now has **81 distinct direct counterparties**, excluding NVIDIA itself. This follow-up adds 13 company/legal-entity records, 13 relationship records, 61 atomic claims, 26 project roles and six listed-instrument mappings. One existing CoreWeave equity record is refined with issuer-filing evidence, rather than creating a second investment. The complete graph contains **360 companies/entities, 564 relationships and 13 project/program records**.

The research covers five bounded contexts: independent compute-financing platforms; AIP and Aligned; Helix; CoreWeave's capacity expansion; and Nebius's capacity expansion. These include investment programs and an acquisition transaction, not five individually verified physical factories. Named participants were enumerated before selected document follow-up. No stock performance was used to select them.

These connections represent different potential economic channels. A financing MOU, a purchase obligation, an issued security, a fund commitment and completed acquisition are different facts. Adding their headline amounts would misrepresent both scale and exposure.

## 1. Six-institution financing: an MOU is still conditional

The [10 August NVIDIA announcement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-partners-with-apollo-blackrock-blackstone-brookfield-goldman-sachs-and-kkr-to-establish-ai-compute-infrastructure-financing-platforms-to-mobilize-over-500-billion-of-third-party-capital) names Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR. It describes MOUs and a collective objective exceeding USD500bn of third-party capital over time, subject to final agreements.

Four newly mapped direct company pairs are NVIDIA–Apollo, NVIDIA–Blackstone, NVIDIA–Goldman Sachs and NVIDIA–KKR. BlackRock's existing record receives additional evidence. Brookfield's precise MOU signing entity is unresolved: BAM represents the group in the project roster, while BN, BAM and investment vehicles remain distinct. There is no new direct NVIDIA–BAM contract edge.

Individual commitments, funded capital, final vehicles, fees and project allocations are unknown. The aggregate target is stored once at program level. It is not NVIDIA capex or a USD500bn commitment from each institution.

**Implication:** financing availability is a plausible dependency to investigate. This announcement alone does not identify funded GPU orders, realized revenue, loan losses or a measurable share-price delay.

## 2. AIP and Aligned: distinguish the program from the acquisition

BlackRock's [19 March 2025 release](https://www.blackrock.com/corporate/newsroom/media/press-releases/ai-infrastructure-partnership) distinguishes NVIDIA's membership/technical-advisor role and GE Vernova/NextEra energy collaboration. AIP's USD30bn initial equity objective and up-to USD100bn investment potential including debt describe different levels of financing. They are not two additive equity commitments.

The [15 October acquisition announcement](https://www.global-infra.com/news/ai-infrastructure-partnership-aip-mgx-and-blackrocks-global-infrastructure-partners-gip-to-acquire-all-equity-in-aligned-data-centers/) expected a first-half-2026 close. The [21 July 2026 official completion announcement](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/technology/mgx-aip-and-blackrocks-gip-complete-acquisition-of-aligned-data-centers/) reports collective acquisition of all Aligned equity, approximately USD40bn enterprise value and an additional USD5bn growth-capital commitment. [Macquarie confirms completion as seller-side fund manager](https://www.macquarie.com/sg/en/about/news/2026/macquarie-asset-management-announces-successful-close-of-aligned-data-centers-transaction.html).

The later announcement replaces the earlier expected timing as an observed milestone. It does not establish cash deployment of the entire growth commitment or individual consortium ownership percentages. Capacity totals include operating and planned assets.

The record separates program founders, energy collaborators, financial anchors, acquisition participants, the selling manager and the target group. NVIDIA's program role does not become a direct Aligned ownership percentage. GE Vernova and NextEra participation does not become an Aligned-specific equipment order or power contract.

Identity checks also matter. [Aligned's legal page](https://aligneddc.com/legal/) identifies a website operator, Aligned Data Centers LLC, and separately names Aligned Energy Holdings LP in purchase terms. The exact equity holding target remains unresolved. [ADGM's register](https://www.adgm.com/public-registers/fsra/firms/financial-firms/mgx-fund-management-limited-240067) verifies MGX Fund Management Limited; that does not identify every MGX transaction SPV.

**Implication:** an acquisition close is stronger execution evidence than a fundraising target, but enterprise value, equity paid, individual stakes and growth funding remain separate measures.

## 3. Helix: the manager, platform and legal fund differ

[Vistra's quarterly release](https://investor.vistracorp.com/2026-08-07-Vistra-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results) names the Helix participants and Vistra's preferred-power role. [Its 10-Q, Note 7 and liquidity discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000169281926000019/vistra-20260630.htm) identifies the specific KKR Helix C LP agreement: an initial USD500m callable commitment and another USD500m conditional on power milestones or Vistra's election. The initial tranche remained callable at 30 June; later calls are unknown.

KKR affiliates manage that fund. This is distinct from owning all its assets. NVIDIA's involvement in the broader Helix program does not prove it invests through Vistra's particular LP vehicle. A preferred-power-provider role does not specify a plant-level PPA.

The [SEC index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1692819/000169281926000019/0001692819-26-000019-index.html) shows 7 August evening acceptance and an official filing date of 10 August. This batch conservatively uses 10 August for the detailed terms, rather than the June contract month or quarter end.

**Implication:** capital calls, financing milestones and power delivery can create different event dates. Commitments cannot be substituted for deployed cash.

## 4. CoreWeave: hardware customer, cloud provider and equity recipient

The [January 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000044/crwv-20260123.htm) confirms issuance on 23 January of 22,935,780 Class A shares to NVIDIA for USD2bn cash, at USD87.20 per share. [Public filing was 26 January](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000044/0001769628-26-000044-index.htm). The existing equity relationship is refined; the event date is not used as an earlier public signal. Its more-than-5GW-by-2030 framework remains forward-looking.

The separate [September 2025 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962825000047/crwv-20250909.htm) discloses a residual-capacity order with an initial USD6.3bn value. NVIDIA's obligation extends through 13 April 2032, subject to delivery, availability and termination conditions. That amount is not immediate cash paid or recognized revenue.

[CoreWeave's definitive proxy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769628/000176962826000191/crwv-20260422.htm) reports NVIDIA MSA/order payments of approximately USD326.3m for FY2025 and USD59.6m for Q1 2026. These are not attributed exclusively to the residual-capacity order. It also reports NVIDIA at 17% of FY2025 supplier purchases while customer contracts required NVIDIA GPUs. Purchase share and GPU dependence are different measures; OEM procurement channels matter.

**Implication:** a demand shock, financing shock and cloud-utilization shock can affect the same pair differently. A single generic supplier/customer edge would conceal those mechanisms.

## 5. Nebius: a pre-funded warrant and a separate Citi facility

The [11 March 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926026163/tm268532d1_6k.htm) identifies an issued pre-funded warrant: approximately USD2bn gross proceeds, 21,065,936 underlying Class A shares and USD0.0001 exercise price per share. The [executed exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1513845/000110465926052948/nbis-20251231xex4d7.htm) supplies further terms. This is a different transaction from the earlier Nebius financing already in the atlas.

[NVIDIA's 13G](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000062/xslSCHEDULE_13G_X01/primary_doc.xml) reports 22,256,412 deemed beneficial Class A shares for the 13 July event, including 21,065,936 unexercised warrant shares, representing 9.3% of that class. [Filing occurred 20 July](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000062/0001045810-26-000062-index.html). This is not an October holding, total-company voting percentage or proof of exercise. The filing's pre-September exercise restriction remains alongside the earlier generic provisions; later exercise is unresolved.

The [22 October Citi facility announcement](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-secures-3-5-billion-financing-facility-with-citi) describes USD3.5bn senior secured capacity, a 364-day tenor, initial Term SOFR margin of 275bp rising to a stated maximum 350bp, with adjustments. Citi's arranger, agent and security-agent roles are recorded separately. Drawn amounts, exact bank affiliate and site allocations remain unknown. No NVIDIA guarantee is inferred.

**Implication:** equity-linked financing, deemed ownership, debt availability and capacity deployment need separate timestamps and outcome definitions. The planned [more-than-5GW-by-end-2030 program](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nvidia-and-nebius-partner-to-scale-full-stack-ai-cloud) is not already operational capacity.

## 6. Research coverage and limits

The root's prior three-annual/two-interim relevant-section reviews were explicitly reused. Six others remain access-limited or partial: Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, Vistra, NextEra, Citi and Nebius. Available cover extracts and transaction filings do not count as full annual reviews. Standalone annuals for private funds/subsidiaries were not obtained.

The source registry records 40 original URLs attempted, with 14 local caches. A failed cache is distinct from an original document successfully read through the web tool. Blocked full annuals remain blocked. Publication dates that were not established remain null; fiscal periods and website copyright years are not substituted. Daily information-cutoff filters are eligibility gates, not precise intraday event clocks. Filing-index locators retain acceptance times; intraday use still requires UTC conversion, dissemination checks and target-market trading eligibility.

BlackRock's annual identifies GIP as Global Infrastructure Management LLC, wholly owned by Saturn Subco, a consolidated BlackRock subsidiary. These are separately modelled legal entities sharing the BLK confirmation group. The exact hierarchy uses undated-source availability and is excluded from historical filters. Consolidation alone does not identify every immediate holding tier or client-fund ownership.

xAI continuity, sovereign-authority roles, exact fund/SPV identities, Brookfield's separately named BAIIF program and the discovered NAVER financing lead remain queued. They were not forced into this batch. BAIIF, AIP and the six-institution platform are not treated as one fund. Named lists, exclusions, source locators and open frontier tasks preserve the search trail.

All new links have explicit primary-source claims. Program participation creates no all-pairs contracts, transitive supplier links, inferred guarantees or undisclosed materiality. This work improves economic hypotheses; it does not demonstrate price transmission, latency or an implementable trading rule.
